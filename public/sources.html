SOURCES & METHODOLOGY

Sources & methodology

This is an independent research archive in development. It combines a recording inventory with an expanding set of imported tracklists and separately sourced historical annotations. It does not yet contain a verified catalog of every Grateful Dead concert.

What the numbers mean

LabelMeaning
Reported dateA group of Archive recordings reporting the same date. Early/late shows can be combined. Dates and venue labels can be wrong. This count is not a concert census.
Recording sourceA distinct Internet Archive identifier. Many recordings may describe one performance.
Tracklist importedFile titles from one selected recording, potentially incomplete. We prefer the imported recording with the most identifiable track titles. We do not infer set boundaries or segues from filenames.
Song playedA title found in that representative recording’s tracklist. Reprises and duplicate recordings count once per date group. Curated aliases connect reviewed title variants. Unreviewed labels remain visible in track evidence and the coverage report, and are excluded from song charts.
Verified complete setlistA separate, cited historical record marked complete after review. The initial release has none. The import format supports these when a reusable catalog is secured.

Frequency is sample evidence

Song charts divide date groups containing a listed title by date groups with imported song evidence in the selected period. Index-only dates are excluded from that denominator. The import selects a reviewed recording for each reported date and can add alternate sources when titles are missing. Coverage expands as metadata is processed. The imported recordings are not a verified census of concerts. Partial tapes, title mismatches and missing imports can all distort a chart.

A blank year means there may be no imported track evidence. It does not mean the band stopped playing a song. The first listed performance in this sample is never presented as a verified debut. Selected debut dates in the history timeline are independently cited.

Ratings describe a recording

Stars and review counts come from Internet Archive’s search index at import time. Reviews may concern music, sound quality, or both. The explorer does not combine review pools across tapes, and it does not count Relisten as a second independent rating source.

Adjusted rating = (average × review count + prior mean × 20) ÷ (review count + 20). The prior mean is the review-count-weighted mean of valid rated items in the inventory. Ranking defaults to a minimum of five reviews and selects one eligible recording per date group. Review counts and search averages can lag behind current pages. Neither raw nor adjusted ratings establish an objective “best era.”

History is another layer

Era filters are editorial and may overlap. Personnel bars describe membership or recurring guest windows, with day, month or year precision recorded in the source data. They do not confirm attendance at every show. Bruce Hornsby is listed as a recurring guest. The touring hiatus includes 1975 performances.

Where the evidence comes from

  • Internet Archive’s Grateful Dead collection: the recording inventory and track metadata, accessed through its metadata API. No audio is copied or hosted. The inventory also contains studio, rehearsal, and uncertain-date material; these require review before it can serve as a concert census. Explicit recording overrides exclude identified radio, studio, and other-artist segments from song counts.
  • Dead.net, official artist archives, and cited label/artist histories: short original historical annotations. Each timeline entry links to its source.
  • Jerrybase and community historical catalogs: candidates for a future verified event and setlist backbone. No bulk catalog copied pending an appropriate reusable export.
  • setlist.fm: not used as a permanent dataset because its standard terms constrain data retention.
  • Relisten: date links provided for listening. API mapping and rating provenance remain under investigation.

Visual identity

The animated rose is an original procedural drawing built from petal, stem and leaf curves. It does not trace existing band artwork.

Reproducible, correctable, expanding

The importer caches public metadata, identifies itself, makes sequential requests, backs off after failures, and can resume. The public snapshot contains recording identifiers, dates, venues, locations, rating summaries, and curated song identities in track order. Unmatched tracks retain a status without the uploader’s text. Raw responses and their provenance are kept locally; generated records are available as JSON and in a local SQLite build.

Official concert releases

The show table includes all 36 original Dick’s Picks volumes and a growing selection of standalone concert albums. Associations use reported performance dates and can include bonus tracks or selections. Same-date early and late shows may still be grouped. A blank release cell means status is not documented here, rather than that no official release exists. Later reissues and separately packaged bonus discs are not exhaustively indexed.

Sources: Grateful Dead Family Discography: Dick’s Picks and Grateful Dead album discography, with individual release links in each table cell. Cross-checks for volumes 17, 22, and 29 resolve date discrepancies and hidden bonus material. This is an independent fan index.

Album markers

Album markers use single solid colors. No album-cover image files are hosted.

Setlist search

The setlist search uses a separate normalized Internet Archive source snapshot. Openers require explicit set boundaries. Early and late performances are searched separately. Without set labels, only explicitly connected source passages can match. Missing titles and cuts limit matches, and an empty result does not establish that a sequence was never played. Source annotations remain subject to review; these are not audio-verified complete concerts.
